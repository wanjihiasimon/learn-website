CISA Domain 4 – Information Systems Operations & Business Resilience

Inventory management ensures visibility of hardware, software, and licensing.

  • Track asset owner, location, status, lifecycle, and classification.
  • Configuration management documents baseline, versions, and relationships.
  • Change/configuration control ensures authorized changes are tested and recorded.

Ensures system meets business needs in performance and resiliency.

  • Monitor CPU, memory, storage, network usage.
  • Plan capacity based on trends and business growth.
  • Enforce availability through redundancy and fault-tolerant designs.
  • Central scheduling systems manage batch processes and workflows.
  • System interfaces ensure secure, accurate data transfers between systems.
  • Monitor failures, retries, and exceptions with alerting and auditing.
  • Incident management restores normal service ASAP using ITIL framework.
  • Problem management identifies root causes to prevent recurrence.
  • Change management ensures controlled updates (CAB reviews, rollback plans).
  • Patch management updates systems securely and timely.

Defines and measures IT service performance and accountability:

  • SLAs with external clients, OLAs internally.
  • Track KPIs: uptime, response, resolution times.
  • Use dashboards and monitoring to validate compliance.

Logs support troubleshooting, compliance, and forensic analysis:

  • Collect logs from servers, apps, databases, and networks.
  • Secure log storage and retention per policy.
  • Use alerting for anomalies (e.g., multiple failures).

Ensures data accuracy, integrity, and availability:

  • DBMS backup/recovery, performance tuning, indexing.
  • Manage schema changes, user access, and lifecycle.
  • Validate data quality: completeness, accuracy, retention policies.

Identifies critical processes and recovery priorities:

  • Assess financial, operational, reputational impacts of outages.
  • Define Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO).
  • Involve business owners and update annually.
  • BCP defines strategies to maintain operations during disruptions.
  • DRP focuses on IT recovery, including alternate sites (hot/warm/cold).
  • Include communications, pandemic, cyber-attack response.
  • Testing: tabletop, structured walk-through, parallel/full interruption tests.
  • Validate RTO/RPO assumptions and infrastructure readiness.
  • Review test results and remediate gaps.

🧠 Flashcards

Q: Difference between RPO and RTO?

A: RTO = max downtime; RPO = max data loss tolerated.

Q: What is the purpose of SLA vs OLA?
Reveal: Show Answer

A: SLA = external agreement; OLA = internal support agreement.
