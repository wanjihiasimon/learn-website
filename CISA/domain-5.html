CISA Domain 5 – Protection of Information Assets

  • Standards: ISO 27001, NIST CSF, COBIT
  • Policies include Acceptable Use, Data Classification, Access Control
  • Governance: Regular review, policy awareness, disciplinary enforcement
  • Perimeter: Fences, lighting, CCTV
  • Access control: Badges, biometrics, visitor logs
  • Environmental: HVAC, fire suppression, water sensors, UPS
  • Firewalls, IDS/IPS, segmentation (VLANs, DMZ)
  • VPNs with strong authentication and encryption
  • Network monitoring and anomaly detection
  • Anti-malware, host-based firewalls, EDR
  • Patch management, secure config, device hardening
  • Mobile device management (MDM), USB control
  • RBAC, ABAC, least privilege, segregation of duties
  • Authentication: MFA, biometric, PKI
  • User lifecycle: onboarding, provisioning, deprovisioning, reviews
  • Algorithms: AES, RSA, ECC, SHA
  • Key lifecycle, HSMs, certificate authorities
  • Data-at-rest and in-transit encryption
  • Laws: GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA
  • Controls: DLP tools, masking, tokenization
  • Data retention, disposal (shredding, crypto erase)
  • SIEM, log correlation, threat intel
  • IR lifecycle: preparation, detection, containment, eradication, recovery, lessons learned
  • Incident response team, communication plans, forensic readiness
  • Integration with BCP/DRP
  • Crisis management, communication, and recovery team roles
  • Redundancy, failover, backups, testing (tabletop & full)
  • Phishing, ransomware, IoT risks, cloud vulnerabilities
  • Security awareness training, phishing simulations
  • Continuous improvement via metrics & assessments
