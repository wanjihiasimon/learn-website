CISA Domain 1: Information System Auditing Process

Defines the purpose, authority, and responsibility of the audit function.

Outlines general, performance, and reporting standards by ISACA.

Guides ethical behavior including confidentiality and integrity.

Involves defining scope, objectives, and identifying resources and risks.

Focuses audit resources on areas of highest risk.

Defines the overall scope of audit coverage and objectives per engagement.

Must be sufficient, reliable, relevant, and useful to support audit conclusions.

Workpapers that support findings, conclusions, and audit opinion.

Computer-Assisted Audit Techniques improve efficiency and depth of testing.

  • Compliance
  • Financial
  • Operational
  • IS Audit
  • Integrated

Communicating audit findings and tracking corrective actions.

Ensures audit work quality through oversight and review.

Internal and external reviews of the audit function.

Auditors may rely on qualified third parties under controlled conditions.
