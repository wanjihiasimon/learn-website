CISA Domain 3: Information Systems Acquisition, Development, and Implementation

Evaluates technical, operational, and financial viability of a proposed system. Ensures alignment with business goals and provides justification for investment.

Establishes project roles, responsibilities, timelines, and stakeholder engagement through frameworks like PRINCE2 or PMBOK.

SDLC includes planning, analysis, design, development, testing, implementation, and maintenance. It ensures structure and quality across IT project phases.

Controls modifications to systems or infrastructure to minimize risk. Includes change request, review, approval, testing, and rollback plans.

Embedded in applications to ensure input accuracy, processing integrity, and output reliability (e.g. validation checks, authorization).

Includes in-house development, outsourcing, commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) systems, or Software-as-a-Service (SaaS). Selection depends on risk, cost, and control needs.

Assesses whether the system met objectives, stayed within budget/scope, and identifies improvements or lessons learned for future projects.

Ensures system meets requirements and works as intended. Includes unit, system, integration, user acceptance (UAT), and regression testing.

Includes business sponsors, steering committees, QA teams, developers, and end users. Each plays a role in ensuring project success and governance.

Flashcard

Q: What are the key phases in the SDLC?

A: Planning, analysis, design, development, testing, implementation, and maintenance.
